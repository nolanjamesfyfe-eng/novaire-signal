## Carl Jung: unconscious / fate

Displayed popular wording: “Until you make the unconscious conscious, it will direct your life and you will call it fate.”

This is not a verified verbatim quotation. Export label: **Paraphrase · Aion, §126**.

Related original passage: “The psychological rule says that when an inner situation is not made conscious, it happens outside, as fate.”

Reference: Jung, *Aion*, Collected Works 9ii, paragraph 126; reproduced and contrasted with the popular wording at https://carljungdepthpsychologysite.blog/2022/02/08/fate-20/ .

HOW THE SCORE WORKS

Seven equal components: sleep, energy, focus, low stress, calm, happiness and movement adherence. Sleep reaches full credit at 8 hours. Stress is reversed, so 0 is best. Movement rewards the day’s appropriate plan—including recovery—not exercise intensity.
